# ── Vegetable Crop Diseases
# Source: TNAU Agritech Portal / UAS Bengaluru

## Tomato Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

### Symptoms
- Dark brown to black lesions with concentric rings (target-board pattern)
- Yellow halos around lesions
- Lesions on older/lower leaves first
- Severe defoliation; fruit lesions with leathery, sunken spots

### Management
- Remove and destroy infected plant debris
- Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2 g/L
- Spray at 10-day intervals during rainy season
- Use disease-free transplants; grow tolerant varieties

## Tomato Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)

### Symptoms
- Water-soaked, irregular lesions on leaves turning brown
- White sporulation visible on underside of leaves in humid conditions
- Brown, firm lesions on green fruits
- Rapid plant collapse in cool, wet weather

### Management
- Metalaxyl-M + Mancozeb @ 2.5 g/L
- Cymoxanil + Mancozeb @ 2.5 g/L
- Spray preventively during cool/rainy weather
- Avoid overhead irrigation; improve drainage

## Chilli Anthracnose (Colletotrichum spp.)

### Symptoms
- Circular, water-soaked lesions on green and ripe fruits
- Lesions turn brown to dark with orange-pink spore masses
- Premature fruit drop

### Management
- Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L or Thiophanate-methyl @ 1.5 g/L
- Hot water seed treatment (52°C for 30 minutes)
- Avoid injuries during harvest

# ── Cotton Pest and Disease Management
# Source: ICAR – Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR)

## Cotton Bollworm (Helicoverpa armigera)

### Damage
- Larvae bore into bolls, squares, and young shoots
- Round holes in bolls with frass
- Heavy boll shedding reduces yield significantly

### IPM Strategy
1. Install pheromone traps @ 5/ha for monitoring (threshold: 8–10 moths/trap/week)
2. Spray NPV (Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus) @ 250 LE/ha at early larval stage
3. Release Trichogramma chilonis @ 1,50,000/ha/week (egg parasitoid)
4. Chemical: Spinosad 45% SC @ 0.3 ml/L or Emamectin benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L

## Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV)

### Symptoms
- Curling and cupping of leaves upward or downward
- Thickening and darkening of veins
- Enation (leaf-like outgrowths) on leaf undersurface
- Stunting; no boll formation in severe cases

### Management
- Use Bt cotton or CLCuD-tolerant varieties
- Control whitefly (vector): Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 ml/L (seed treatment preferred)
- Avoid high nitrogen; remove and destroy infected plants

# ── Soil Health and Fertilizer Management
# Source: ICAR – Indian Institute of Soil Science (IISS) / Soil Health Card Scheme

## Importance of Soil Health Cards
The Indian Government's Soil Health Card (SHC) scheme provides:
- NPK status
- Secondary nutrients (S, Ca, Mg)
- Micronutrients (Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B)
- Soil pH and electrical conductivity

Contact your nearest KVK or soil testing laboratory for a free soil health card.

## Zinc Deficiency (Very Common in Indian Soils)

### Symptoms
- Khaira disease in rice (pale yellow-brown streaks and spots)
- Brown rust-like spots on rice leaves
- Interveinal chlorosis in maize and vegetables
- Delayed maturity

### Correction
- Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4·7H2O) @ 25 kg/ha as basal application
- Foliar spray: 0.5% ZnSO4 + 0.25% lime solution

## Iron Deficiency

### Symptoms
- Interveinal chlorosis on young/upper leaves (stays green along veins)
- Common in calcareous (high pH) soils

### Correction
- Ferrous Sulphate 1% foliar spray (2–3 sprays at weekly intervals)
- Soil application less effective in alkaline soils

# ── Government Schemes for Farmers (India)
# Source: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

## PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- Direct income support: ₹6,000/year in 3 installments of ₹2,000
- Eligibility: Small and marginal farmer families with cultivable land
- Register at pmkisan.gov.in or nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)

## Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- Crop insurance scheme
- Premium: 2% for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi, 5% for horticulture
- Covers yield losses due to natural calamities, pests, diseases
- Enroll through banks or state agriculture department

## eNAM (National Agriculture Market)
- Online trading platform for agricultural commodities
- Helps farmers get better prices by connecting to buyers
- Register at enam.gov.in

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
- Provides short-term credit for crop production
- Interest subvention of 2%; additional 3% for prompt repayment
- Apply through nationalized banks, cooperative banks, or RRBs

# ── Organic Farming Practices
# Source: ICAR – National Centre of Organic Farming (NCOF)

## Benefits of Organic Farming
- Improves soil health and biodiversity
- Reduces chemical input costs
- Premium prices for certified organic produce
- Environmentally sustainable

## Key Organic Inputs

### Vermicompost
- Prepare using earthworms (Eisenia fetida)
- Application: 5–10 tonnes/ha
- Rich in plant-available N, P, K and beneficial microbes

### Jeevamrit / Panchagavya
- Traditional biostimulants used in Vedic farming
- Improves microbial activity in soil

### Neem-based Products
- Neem cake: 250 kg/ha as soil application (pest repellent + fertilizer)
- Neem oil 3% (NSKE – Neem Seed Kernel Extract) for pest control

## Certification
Contact APEDA (apeda.gov.in) or state organic certification bodies for Participatory Guarantee System (PGS) or third-party certification.
